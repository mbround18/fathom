# Sponsorship & Funding Policy

Uwusquad (Fathom, Depths, Keel, Ballast, and the shared portal) is free and open source, and stays
that way — no paid tier, no feature gating. Donations fund maintenance, not access, and cover
infrastructure, test environments, security hardening, and the hours spent building and supporting
the suite.

## Approved & Preferred Funding Platforms

- **GitHub Sponsors:** [`mbround18`](https://github.com/sponsors/mbround18)
- **Ko-fi:** [`mbround18`](https://ko-fi.com/mbround18)
- **PayPal:** [paypal.me/MichaelBruno](https://paypal.me/MichaelBruno)

## Vetting & Outside Funding Sources

Any external funding source, alternative payment processor, or sponsorship method outside the
platforms listed above is vetted individually. Sources that require joining a proprietary
platform or community solely to receive funds are declined, to avoid implicit endorsements or
transactional obligations.

## Thank You!

Whether you sponsor via GitHub, drop a tip on Ko-fi, contribute code, or just star the repo —
your support keeps the Uwusquad suite alive, secure, and free.
